About BDOG

Built different.

No endless generic modification lists. Start with the car, the goal and the budget, then make sensible decisions from there.

Real cars. Real parts. Real people.

BDOG Automotive is being built around the idea that modifying a car should be easier to understand. The builder brings the vehicle, goals and budget together so the owner can see a sensible route forward.

The next layer is community: owners sharing builds, experiences and recommendations, with a growing network of garages that can turn those plans into finished cars.
