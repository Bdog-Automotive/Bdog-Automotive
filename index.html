How it works

Build your car properly.

Four simple steps: identify the car, choose what you want, set the budget and get a build plan you can actually use.

01

Your car

Enter your registration and BDOG will identify the vehicle, or choose the make and model manually. This gives the build a proper starting point.

02

Your goals

Pick what matters to you — more power, better handling, better sound, better looks, fast-road performance or track use.

03

Your budget

Set a realistic parts budget so the recommendations stay useful. You can always change the budget before creating the final build.

04

Your build

BDOG brings the choices together into a clear build plan, with suggested parts, estimated costs and reasons for the recommendations.
